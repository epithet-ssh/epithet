Implement rare managed host-ID replacement and administrative recovery without coupling principal identity to routine SSH transport-key rotation. Acceptance: ordinary SSH host-key rotation leaves the host ID and derived principals unchanged; planned host-ID replacement makes targets accept old and new derivations, switches issuance to the new ID, and removes the old ID after the maximum certificate lifetime; emergency recovery can revoke the old derivation immediately; clone and alias conflicts remain explicit and audited throughout the transition.
