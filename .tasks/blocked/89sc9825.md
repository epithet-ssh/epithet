Source: API audit thbfp6pt (.tasks/closed/thbfp6pt.md). User requested high-priority remediation tasks; filing does not implement the proposed changes.

Problem: PolicyRequest embeds the entire inventory Resolution envelope, including inventory protocol version, resolvedAt, and revisions. resolvedAt is only checked for presence; it has no freshness or audit use. Policy echoes directory/inventory revisions, and CA then overwrites them from its original lookup.

After settling policy output ownership, define policy input from the normalized authentication, user, host, and connection facts actually required for evaluation. Keep inventory transport metadata and private audit context owned by CA. Remove resolvedAt and redundant revision echoes. Do not remove directory/host revisions from inventory-to-CA resolution or issuance logs.

Acceptance:
- Policy no longer depends on the inventory transport envelope/version or echoes inventory revisions.
- CA retains the exact directory/host revisions used for the decision and combines them with policy content ID for logging.
- No user bearer token or provider settings reach policy; no audit revisions reach public clients or certificates.
- Preserve authentication expiry, identity and target binding, explicit missing records, and accounts:null versus accounts:[] semantics.
- Update API schemas and tests; static content revisions remain meaningful for later dynamic snapshot versions.
